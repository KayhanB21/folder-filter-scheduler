Safer defaults for new actions, and To and Cc in address-book conditions.
  • A new rule, or a new action, no longer starts on "Move to Trash". It starts on "Choose an action…", and the rule cannot be saved until you pick one, so mail is never trashed by an action you did not choose
  • Address-book conditions can now check To and Cc as well as From, Reply-To and Sender. They match when any recipient is in the chosen book
